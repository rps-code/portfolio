:::project-section{type="text"}
I built this website for my good friend’s charity car owners group, North West Imprezas, where I also help marshal events. Using Nuxt 4, I took the opportunity to work with the latest framework while creating a clean, modern design that highlights the group’s amazing charity work. I tailored the site to my friend’s needs, making it both user-friendly and visually appealing, even while working with a challenging colour scheme, all while capturing the spirit of the car club.
