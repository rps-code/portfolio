:::project-section{type="text"}
Hover Cursor is a lightweight JavaScript library that lets you replace the standard mouse pointer with unique, animated cursors for your website. It’s built for developers and designers who want to bring more personality and interactivity to their user experience.

With full styling control, smooth animations, and easy setup, it’s a simple way to make hover interactions stand out. I created this to experiment with branding and micro-interactions, giving websites a custom feel without overcomplicating the process.
