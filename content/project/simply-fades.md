:::project-section{type="text"}
Simply Fades is a tiny but powerful animation library designed to make elements fade in from either the left or right as they enter the viewport. It’s lightweight, easy to use, and perfect for adding subtle motion to your site without the bloat.

Ideal for developers who want quick, plug-and-play scroll animations that feel natural and smooth. I created this a while back to enhance the user experience on my projects without relying on heavy libraries. It’s all about simplicity and performance with no unnecessary complexity.
